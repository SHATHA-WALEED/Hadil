A letter from me

Dearest Hadil,

Happy birthday to you, my beautiful human. ♡

I don't really know how to begin this because every time I try to put into words what you mean to me, the words somehow feel too small.

It's funny, isn't it? How someone can live so far away, on the other side of a screen, and yet somehow find a way to become such a real and beautiful part of your life.

I never expected an online friendship to mean this much to me. I never expected a person I met through a screen to become someone whose presence could make my days softer, my laughs louder, and my heart a little happier.

But then there was you. And somehow, little by little, you became one of those people I never want to lose.

You are genuinely one of the best people I've ever had the chance to know. And I hope you know that when I say that, I mean it with every part of my heart.

I love the way you are you. The way you talk. The way you laugh. The little things you probably don't even realize you do. The way you can make an ordinary conversation feel like something I'll remember. The way your presence can make a bad day feel a little less heavy.

There are people who enter our lives like passing seasons. And then there are people who quietly become part of the landscape of our hearts. You're the second kind.

You are one of those rare people that I feel incredibly lucky to have found. And if life is really just a collection of tiny coincidences, then meeting you is one of my favorite coincidences ever.

I wish I could give you something beautiful today. Something I could wrap in ribbons, put in a box, and hand to you while telling you how much you mean to me. But there's one tiny problem... you're far away.

So I thought, okay, if I can't bring the gift to her, maybe I can build one for her. And that's how this little corner of the internet came to exist.

It isn't expensive. It isn't wrapped in fancy paper. It probably isn't even the most impressive gift you'll ever receive. But every little part of it was made with you in mind, because I wanted to give you something that says: I thought about you. Not just for a few minutes, but enough to sit down and create something that exists because you do.

I wanted you to have a little place you can come back to someday and remember that there was someone, somewhere, who was genuinely so happy that you existed. And I hope that whenever life gets overwhelming, whenever you doubt yourself, whenever you feel like you're not doing enough, or whenever the world feels a little too loud... you remember this: you are loved, you are appreciated, you are important, and you make a difference in people's lives simply by being yourself. Including mine.

I hope this new year of your life brings you the kind of happiness that doesn't need to be forced. The kind that sneaks up on you while you're doing something ordinary. The kind that makes you stop for a second and think, "wow... I'm really happy."

I hope you meet people who appreciate your heart. I hope you accomplish the things you've been quietly wishing for. I hope you have reasons to smile on the days you thought you wouldn't. I hope you discover new places, hear new songs, make ridiculous memories, laugh until your stomach hurts, and experience moments you'll want to keep forever.

And when things don't go perfectly, as they never do, I hope you remember that one bad chapter doesn't get to decide the whole story. You still have so many beautiful pages left to write.

So here's to another year of you. Another year of your laughter, your dreams, your chaos, your random conversations, your little stories, your beautiful heart, and all the things that make you uniquely you. And selfishly, I hope I get to be there for some of those pages.

I hope our friendship keeps growing in all the strange and beautiful ways friendships do. Maybe one day we'll laugh about how ridiculous it is that we started as two people talking through screens. Maybe one day we'll finally meet and realize that after all those messages and calls and conversations, we were even better friends in real life.

Until then, I'll happily keep being your friend from my little corner of the world. Because distance might mean I can't knock on your door, it might mean I can't show up with a birthday cake today, it might mean I can't give you a hug today — but it doesn't mean I can't celebrate you. And believe me, I'm celebrating you.

May this year be gentle with your heart. May it surprise you with beautiful things. May you become the person you've always wanted to be. And may you never forget how incredibly special you are.

Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. Thank you for becoming my friend, and for making a tiny corner of my world brighter just by being in it.

If I could give you one thing today, it wouldn't be money, flowers, or anything wrapped in a box. I'd give you the ability to see yourself through the eyes of the people who love you. Because then you'd finally understand what I see when I look at you: someone incredibly, incredibly worth knowing.

Happy birthday, you wonderful human. 🤍 Here's to you. Here's to us. And here's to all the memories we haven't made yet. I hope this is only the beginning.

with so much love,
your friend from behind the screen ♡
